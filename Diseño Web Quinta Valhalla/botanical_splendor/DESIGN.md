## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a balance of natural sophistication, warm luxury, and organic harmony for an exclusive garden estate venue. Catered toward discerning couples, executive event planners, and private hosts seeking memorable celebrations, the interface evokes serenity, timeless hospitality, and understated grandeur.

The visual style merges **Warm Luxury** with **Organic Minimalism**. Soft, tactile cream backdrops replace sterile digital whites, layered with rich botanical accents, generous negative space, golden atmospheric glows, and refined editorial composition. Imagery commands focal attention, supported by restrained typography and subtle physical elevation.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in botanical tones and artisanal warmth:
- **Primary (`#1B3B2B`)**: Deep forest green evoking mature canopy foliage, used for high-impact interactive anchors, primary buttons, and framing containers.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Warm golden earth accentuating badges, active states, keylines, and delicate metallic highlights reminiscent of festive evening illumination.
- **Tertiary (`#5E7153`)**: Muted sage olive representing organic balance, supporting metadata, soft tags, and secondary graphical cues.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FAF8F5`)**: Organic ivory canvas, offering warm readability without harsh glare, complemented by `#F3EFEA` for elevated card surfaces.
- **Body & Text Slate (`#1F2421`)**: Deep charcoal pine tone that maintains strong contrast and editorial softness over body copy.

## Typography
Editorial romance meets crisp usability. Headlines use **Playfair Display** to echo luxury invitation cards and heritage estate character, featuring deliberate letter-spacing and graceful high-contrast strokes. Functional text and interface controls rely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering open counters and gentle geometric curves that harmonize with the rounded aesthetic. Uppercase tracking is applied to category labels and kicker tags to reinforce prestige curation.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column layout on desktop anchors the content within a generous max-width of 1280px, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices. 

Spatial rhythm embraces unhurried luxury: wide outer margins preserve breathing room, while component spacing encourages immersion through expansive photography frames. Content reflow prioritizes stacked media-first cards on viewports narrower than 768px.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is cultivated through **warm ambient diffusion** and **layered surfaces** rather than stark dropshadows:
- **Base Surface**: Warm cream (`#FAF8F5`) layered under container planes (`#F3EFEA` or pure white with 80% opacity).
- **Ambient Shadow (Resting)**: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.08)`. The tinted forest and gold undertone eliminates clinical greys.
- **Ambient Shadow (Elevated / Hover)**: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.12)`.
- **Borders**: Translucent hairline frames (`1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.25)`) to enclose imagery and interactive cards with jewellery-grade precision.

## Shapes
Forms celebrate organic curves and architecture-inspired archways. Default interface elements use a 0.5rem (8px) baseline radius. Elevated interactive surface cards, venue feature showcases, and media containers expand into generous curvature levels (1rem to 1.5rem / `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`), mirroring botanical soft contours and manicured landscape design.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid deep forest green (`#1B3B2B`) fill with warm ivory text (`#FAF8F5`). Border-radius `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg`. Soft hover expansion with subtle ambient green glow.
- **Secondary (Accent)**: Golden earth outline (`#C5A880`) on transparent or cream background, with `#1B3B2B` text. Subtle background tint on hover (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.1)`).
- **Ghost / Editorial**: Plain text with an animated golden underline expanding outward from center.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill silhouettes (`rounded-full`) with `#5E7153` tinted backgrounds (`rgba(94, 113, 83, 0.12)`) and deep sage text. Event classification badges (e.g., "Weddings", "Galas", "Outdoor Garden") use small uppercase styling with generous tracking.

### Cards & Feature Containers
- Enclosed with soft 1.5rem (`rounded-2xl`) corners, hairline borders in muted earth gold (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.3)`), and `#F3EFEA` background fills. Rich photography utilizes smooth 4:3 or 16:9 aspect ratios with an inner subtle gradient overlay for text readability.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Framed in soft warm ivory (`#FFFFFF` with warm cream tint) with a 1px border (`#DCD6CD`). Active focus state transitions smoothly to `#1B3B2B` border with a subtle golden halo ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.2)`). Labels rest comfortably above the field in medium weight.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular and rounded-square outlines in `#5E7153`. Checked state fills with `#1B3B2B` and showcases an ivory checkmark or centered dot, delivering an organic, tactile engagement.

### Specialized Venue Components
- **Booking Date Ribbon**: A multi-step horizontal timeline utilizing slender gold keylines and forest green circular status indicators.
- **Curated Gallery Arch**: High-end media cards topped with semicircular botanical arch crowns, nodding directly to estate architecture.
